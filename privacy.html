Privacy Policy

quotedashai.com/privacy

What we collect

QuoteDash AI is built to work offline. Job details, quotes, and invoices you create stay on your device unless you choose to export or share them.

Account information

  • Email address, if you create an account or contact support
  • Basic device and crash diagnostics, to keep the app working

What we don't do

  • We don't sell your data.
  • We don't read the contents of your quotes or invoices.
  • We don't collect payment card details — QuoteDash AI doesn't process payments.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to the support address listed in the app.
