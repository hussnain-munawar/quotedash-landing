LIVE-DICTATION APPS

QUESTIONS

Before you install

Does this work for switch rooms, basements, and roof cavities with no signal?

Yes, for finishing and sending. No bars in a switch room or basement? Write up the job from your on-device recording by hand and send the PDF the moment you're back in range — that part never needs a connection. The AI auto-fill (turning your voice into priced line items) does need signal to work, but it never stops you getting a quote out the door.

Can I save my standard rates for power points, switchboards, and safety switches?

Yes — the Personal Price Book lets you set a default rate for jobs you quote all the time, like a double power point or a service call. Say it again on a future job and QuoteDash AI recognizes the item and suggests your saved rate automatically.

What if a compressor, saw, or drill kicks in while I'm recording a quote?

QuoteDash AI records the full clip first, then transcribes and extracts it server-side with automatic retry on failure — a bad take gets reprocessed, not retyped from scratch. Live-dictation apps that stream your voice straight into a text field garble the line the moment site noise kicks in.

Can I show a customer a photo of the fault or fitting on the quote?

Yes — attach a photo to any line item, like a damaged run or a scorched outlet, so the customer can see exactly what they're paying for, right on the quote.

Is there an iOS app?

Not yet — QuoteDash AI is Android-only today. iOS is a future direction, not a committed date.
