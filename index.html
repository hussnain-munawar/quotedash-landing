Why record-then-transcribe, not live dictation

Most voice-quoting apps garble the second a compressor kicks in. This one doesn’t.

LIVE-DICTATION APPS

Streams your voice straight into a text field as you talk. A saw, compressor, or gust of wind mid-sentence garbles the line right then — no retry, no reprocessing.

QUOTEDASH AI

Records the full clip first, then transcribes and extracts it server-side with automatic retry on failure. A bad take gets reprocessed, not retyped from scratch.

QUESTIONS

Before you install

What happens after my 10 free quotes or 14-day trial window?

You'll be prompted to choose Starter ($16.99/mo, 40 quotes/month) or Pro ($209/yr or $27.99/mo, unlimited) — no surprise charge, and no card is needed to start your trial.

What if I misspeak a price or quantity while recording?

Just correct yourself out loud before you stop the recording — say the right price or quantity and QuoteDash AI uses the correction, no need to start over. This only works within the same recording; once you've stopped, fix it in the form like any other edit.

Does it really work with no signal?

Yes, for finishing and sending. No bars? Write up the job from your recording by hand and send the PDF the moment you're back in range — that part never needs a connection. The AI auto-fill (turning your voice into priced line items) does need signal to work, but it never stops you getting a quote out the door.

Is my data safe?

QuoteDash AI is built to work offline — job details, quotes, and invoices stay on your device unless you export or share them. We don't sell your data or read the contents of your quotes. Full details in our privacy policy.

Is there an iOS app?

Not yet — QuoteDash AI is Android-only today. iOS is a future direction, not a committed date.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes — both Starter and Pro are standard Google Play subscriptions and can be cancelled anytime from Play Store subscription settings.
